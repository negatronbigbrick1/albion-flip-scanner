How to read results

  • Buy at: the city with the lowest reported sell listing price.
  • Sell at: the city with the highest reported buy order price.
  • Net profit: estimated sell proceeds after the fee minus the buy cost.
  • ROI: estimated net profit divided by the buy cost.
  • Market data is submitted by players and may be delayed, incomplete or missing. Confirm prices, order volume, item quality and fees in-game before trading.
